## Smart Automation for Homes, Offices, and Buildings

Control lighting, fans, pumps, and other compatible equipment through connected switches, schedules, and sensor-based automation. We develop device firmware, control logic, and web or mobile applications around your installation.

### 01 — Smart Switches & Remote Control

Turn compatible lights, fans, and connected appliances on or off through a mobile app. Group controls by room or area while retaining local switching where supported.

**Useful for: Homes, offices, and commercial spaces.**

### 02 — Automatic Water Pump Control

Start or stop a water pump based on tank levels, configured schedules, or an authorized manual command. Include suitable level sensors and protection controls for the installation.

**Useful for: Houses, apartment buildings, and office water tanks.**

### 03 — Home Automation

Bring everyday controls into one application. Set up routines to switch selected lights, fans, and appliances together—for example, when leaving home or preparing for the evening.

**Useful for: Houses, apartments, and residential properties.**

### 04 — Office Automation

Schedule selected lighting and equipment around office hours. Create separate controls for workspaces, cabins, and common areas so each zone can follow its own operating routine.

**Useful for: Offices, coworking spaces, and business centres.**

### 05 — Motion-Based Lighting

Switch lights on when movement is detected and off after a configured period without activity. Adjust timing and sensor placement to suit how people use the space.

**Useful for: Corridors, staircases, washrooms, parking areas, and storerooms.**

### 06 — Automatic Fan & Ventilation Control

Operate compatible fans or exhaust systems using temperature, humidity, occupancy, or schedules. Define switching thresholds and provide manual controls for authorized users.

**Useful for: Meeting rooms, utility areas, offices, and residential spaces.**

### 07 — Building & Common-Area Automation

Coordinate selected corridor lights, outdoor lighting, water pumps, and ventilation equipment through a shared control interface. Assign access by responsibility and configure routines for different areas.

**Useful for: Apartment complexes, office buildings, and commercial properties.**

### 08 — Timers & Scheduled Device Operation

Set compatible equipment to run at specific times or for a defined duration. Configure recurring schedules for signage, outdoor lights, fans, or pumps, with operating limits suited to each device.

**Useful for: Homes, offices, shops, and managed buildings.**
